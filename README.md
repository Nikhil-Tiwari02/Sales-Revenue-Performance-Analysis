# Sales & Revenue Performance Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data and presents the results through a professional Microsoft Excel dashboard. The dashboard evaluates revenue, profit, product performance, regional performance, salesperson performance, monthly revenue trends, and category-level profit margins.

## 🎯 Business Problem
A company has sales data containing orders, products, categories, regions, salespeople, revenue, discounts, profit, and quantities. Management needs a clear view of sales performance to identify the strongest products, categories, regions, salespeople, and periods, while also understanding profitability.

## 🎯 Objective
The objective is to analyze sales performance using Microsoft Excel and build a dashboard that provides clear, decision-ready insights into revenue, profit, products, regions, salespeople, and monthly performance.

## 📁 Dataset Description
- **Records:** 100 orders
- **Fields:** 11
- **Products:** 10
- **Categories:** Technology, Furniture, Accessories
- **Regions:** North, South, East, West

### Dataset Fields
| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Order ID | Unique identifier for each order |
| Order Date | Date on which the order was placed |
| Product | Product sold |
| Category | Product category |
| Region | Geographical sales region |
| Salesperson | Person responsible for the sale |
| Quantity | Number of units sold |
| Unit Price | Price per unit |
| Revenue | Total revenue generated from the order |
| Discount | Discount offered on the order |
| Profit | Profit generated from the order |

## 💡 Key Insights
1. **Technology** generated the highest revenue of **₹26,42,335**, contributing approximately **65%** of total revenue.
2. **Accessories** had the highest profit margin at **25%**, followed by Furniture at 20% and Technology at 15%.
3. **Laptop** was the best-performing product by revenue and profit, generating **₹9,15,200 revenue** and **₹1,37,280 profit**.
4. **Amit** generated the highest revenue of **₹12,26,170** among all salespeople, while each salesperson handled **25 orders**.
5. **March** recorded the highest monthly revenue at **₹6,56,700**, while **May** recorded the lowest at **₹5,14,810**.

## 💼 Business Recommendations
- Focus on expanding high-revenue Technology products while monitoring their comparatively lower margin.
- Explore opportunities to increase sales of Accessories because of their strong 25% profit margin.
- Analyze the success factors behind Laptop sales and consider applying them to other products.
- Review performance differences between salespeople to identify and share effective sales practices.
- Investigate the reasons for the lower revenue in May and identify opportunities to reduce seasonal dips.
